Question 4: The Reviews dataset contains one row per customer review and focuses on user feedback and interactions. It includes fields such as the individual rating, review title, review text, reviewer user_id, product identifiers (asin and parent_asin), review timestamp, helpful_vote, verified_purchase, and optional images.  The Reviews Metadata dataset contains one row per product and focuses on product information rather than individual customer comments. It includes fields such as main_category, product title, aggregate average_rating, rating_number, price, features, description, store, categories, details, product media, and parent_asin.  The main difference is the level of analysis: the Reviews dataset describes individual customer experiences, while the metadata dataset describes the products themselves. The two datasets can be connected using parent_asin. An individual review's rating is a single customer's score, whereas average_rating in the metadata is an aggregate product-level score.

Comparing .info() for the Reviews and Metadata DataFrames also made the different purposes and structures of the two datasets clear.
