# Project: Managing Product Development

**Project due on January 19, 2027**

## Submitting Your Project

1. Once you've completed the project, gather the following project deliverables for submission:
   1. Make sure that any links in your slide deck are public and accessible to anyone.
   2. Save your project slide deck as a PDF document.
2. Once you're ready to submit, click the **SUBMIT PROJECT** button and upload your file.

## Submit Project

Submission options:

- **Zip File**
- **Public GitHub Repository**

### Upload Zip File

Compress your project file(s) into a single zip file on your computer. 500 MB limit (uncompressed).

Click **Choose File** to select your zip file.
