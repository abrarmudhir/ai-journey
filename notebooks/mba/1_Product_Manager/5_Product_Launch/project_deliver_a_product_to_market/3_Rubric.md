# Rubric

Use this project rubric to understand and assess the project criteria.

## Define Product and Market

| Criteria | Submission Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Create or update a PRD to identify the users, the problem and the product | PRD is well-organized and includes:<ul><li>Background information about the industry or the problem</li><li>Problem Statement</li><li>Product Goals</li><li>Key Features of your project</li><li>Success Metrics</li><li>Target Market</li></ul> |
| Estimate Size of Total Addressable Market and add it to the PRD | Market size is estimated based on publicly available data<br><br>Size calculation uses reasonable assumptions and is logical<br><br>Data sources and assumptions are documented |
| Identify and assess the size of competitors | At least two competitors are identified<br><br>Each competitor's size is estimated (users, sales or ideally both)<br><br>Sources for data are included and assumptions are documented |

## Marketing and Pricing Strategy

| Criteria | Submission Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Determine acquisition channels | At least three channels are identified in the PRD. At least two are not AppStore channels.<br><br>Each channel has:<ul><li>A description of the channel</li><li>An explanation of why this channel was chosen</li></ul> |
| Provide guidance to the Marketing team | A Marketing Guide is linked in the PRD.<br><br>The Marketing guide includes:<ul><li>Key features of the product</li><li>Benefits to users and consumers</li><li>How to use the product</li><li>Where users can find the product</li></ul> |
| Identify the optimal pricing strategy to meet the financial goal for the product | A Pricing section is added to the PRD including:<ul><li>The revenue goal</li><li>An explanation of why this goal was set</li><li>A pricing strategy to meet the revenue goal</li></ul> |

## Preparing for Launch

| Criteria | Submission Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Develop the Pre-Launch Checklist | The checklist is included in the PRD and includes all of the teams that you will need to interface with before launch<br><br>Each team has at least one discussion item specified |
| Anticipate and plan for risks | At least three risk factors are identified in the PRD<br><br>A mitigation plan is included for each risk factor |
| Provide a training plan for sales, customer support, and technical writers | A training guide for the Sales and Customer Support Teams is linked in the PRD and includes:<ul><li>Key features of the product</li><li>Benefits to users and consumers</li><li>How to use the product</li><li>Where users can find the product</li></ul> |
| Create a user guide | User guide for users/customers is linked in the PRD and describes at least two of the product's key features and explains:<ul><li>How to access each feature</li><li>How to use each feature</li></ul> |

## Post Launch Activities

| Criteria | Submission Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Create a test to determine if a new feature solves a problem identified from post-launch user feedback | The proposed solution and the test are included in the PRD<br><br>The test includes:<ul><li>A description of the proposed solution and an explanation of why it solves the problem</li><li>Success metric for the test</li><li>A description of the control and variant</li><li>A hypothesis about what will happen</li></ul> |
| Write a launch email to the organization | A link to the email is included in the PRD.<br><br>The email includes:<ul><li>Background information about why the product was developed</li><li>A description of the product and its key features</li><li>Visual representations of launch data</li><li>Next steps</li></ul> |

## Suggestions to Make Your Project Stand Out

- Sales guide (or marketing guide) has visual elements and a good guide to product features and how to use the product
- The launch email has nice graphics and more details. Looks more detailed than expected.
- For the A/B testing, create a more sophisticated test with at least three arms in addition to the control.
