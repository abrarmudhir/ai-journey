# Product Scenarios

Choose one of the fictional scenarios below to use for your project.

## LinkedIn

LinkedIn plans to launch a personalized career growth platform that helps professionals identify skill gaps and recommend courses, certifications, and networking opportunities to advance in their careers. The platform will analyze users’ profiles, career goals, and industry trends to offer tailored career development plans, while also enabling companies to find and connect with potential candidates based on emerging skills.

### Field Feedback

Your users are adopting the app, but they are not enrolling in the recommended courses. Only 2% of course views turn into an enrollment. What can you do to improve that?

## Morgan Stanley

Morgan Stanley wants to launch a new feature in their Wealth Management app that automates investment portfolio management for individual investors. The app will assess market trends, personal risk preferences, and financial goals to provide personalized investment strategies.

### Post-launch data

| Week | Number of portfolio adjustments | Average portfolio performance improvement |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 100 | 5% |
| 2 | 300 | 12% |
| 3 | 500 | 10% |
| 4 | 500 | 15% |
| 5 | 600 | 18% |

### Field Feedback

The Morgan Stanley team is generally very happy with your product, but in about 25% of the cases, users requested to speak with an advisor before adjusting their investment portfolio. What can you do to address this issue?

## Hilton

Hilton is developing a virtual concierge feature within its existing mobile app Hilton Honors, allowing guests to request room service, book spa treatments and activities, make dining reservations, and get personalized recommendations for local attractions. The goal is to make the guest experience seamless and increase revenue from concierge services.

### Post-launch data

| Week | Number of users | Average concierge bookings per night | Average concierge revenue per night |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 100 | 0.8 | $40 |
| 2 | 300 | 0.9 | $50 |
| 3 | 500 | 0.9 | $45 |
| 4 | 500 | 1.2 | $60 |
| 5 | 600 | 1.5 | $70 |

### Field Feedback

Hilton is happy with your product, but they report that only 30% of dining reservations are made through the app, compared to 65% for spa reservations and 72% for activities. What can you do about that?
