# Project: Deliver a Product to Market

**Project due on February 13, 2027**

## Submission Requirements

- Updated PRD document
- Marketing Guide
- Training Guide for Sales and Customer Support
- User Guide
- Launch email

**If you submit a Google Doc for any of your documents**, please check the sharing permissions to ensure that the doc and all of the linked documents in your project have permissions set to ***Anyone with the link*** so that the reviewers can access your project.

## Submit Project

Choose **Zip File** or **Project Link** in the submission form.

### Upload Zip File

Compress your project file(s) into a single ZIP file on your computer. The limit is 500 MB uncompressed. Select **Choose File** to upload the ZIP file.
