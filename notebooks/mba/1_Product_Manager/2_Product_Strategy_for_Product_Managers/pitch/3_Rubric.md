# Rubric

Use this project rubric to understand and assess the project criteria.

## Business Case

| Criteria | Submission Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| **Initial Focus**<br><br>Identify problems to solve by creating a compelling story using evidence and metrics | - The problem clearly outlines what is being solved.<br>- The business case articulates what the pain point is.<br>- Specific metrics are cited to back up the claim.<br>- Potential cause(s) are included to back up the claim.<br>- A call to action that resonates with stakeholders is included in the business case. |
| **Opportunity**<br><br>Calculate the total addressable market (TAM) for your product | - The size of the opportunity is quantified.<br>- A piece of the market is being approached, and its size and the reason to target this piece are included.<br>- At least two stats from the research are provided to back up the claim:<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;- One is the **Total Addressable Market**.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;- The other could be competitor revenue, market saturation, market population, or others.<br>- Links and sources should be used where possible.<br><br>**Note:** The market opportunity needs to be smaller and more realistic. TAM should be measured in billions, but the initial focus area should be measured in tens of millions at most. |

## Proposal, ROI, and Measurement

| Criteria | Submission Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| **Proposal**<br><br>Define product use cases and target user(s) | - A brief detail of high-level functionality is stated on what is to be built, just enough for readers to understand the proposal.<br>- A clear solution to the problem is outlined. A reader or viewer should understand what is being proposed to be built.<br><br>**Tip:** The proposal shouldn't be vague, like "I will build a marketplace!" It has to be specific. What kind of marketplace? What does it do? How is it special? What audience does it address? |
| **ROI**<br><br>Calculate the return on investment | - The cost calculation is included.<br>- The revenue or cost savings that can be produced are specified.<br>- The return on investment is calculated.<br><br>**Tip:** Think of the simple calculation while attempting to calculate the cost of development. X months of development * 10k each / some simple measure of return. |
| **Measurement**<br><br>Define success metrics | - The success of the idea is measured.<br>- The goal for the first-year success metrics is identified. |

## Competitors

| Criteria | Submission Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| **Competitors and Advantages**<br><br>Compare the features of competitor products/solutions and define product positioning and opportunity in the market. | - At least one competitor is outlined. A category of the competitor may be highlighted instead of a specific one.<br>- A brief overview of at least four features is included for each competitor. It should be enough so that the audience can understand the offering.<br>- Comparison of the product with other products in the market is outlined and answers the question "why us?" |

## Roadmap and Vision

| Criteria | Submission Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Define and craft compelling vision, strategy and build a roadmap around the identified strategic areas. | - Crafted a compelling vision.<br>- At least two themes are provided corresponding to the future strategy of the product.<br>- For each strategic theme, two or three features are provided related to it, along with a brief explanation of each.<br><br>**Note:** The features under each theme should be related and should be at most three. |

## Presentation & Delivery

| Criteria | Submission Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Create a compelling presentation and be persuasive | - The provided template is used, and the slides applicable to the product are completed and should match the suggested format.<br>- Each slide has a key takeaway, and everything supports that takeaway.<br>- The slides are consistently formatted.<br>- At least three visuals are included, enhancing the slide's meaning.<br>- At least two sources are cited.<br>- At least two numbers obtained from outside sources are presented.<br>- A statement that evoked an emotional response is included in the presentation.<br>- Fact-based data is present. |
| Present a 10-minute overview of the product opportunity to an executive stakeholder. | - The slides are presented as video and with voiceover.<br>- The presentation is under 10 minutes.<br>- Smooth transition is done between bullets and between slides.<br>- The idea summary is narrated instead of directly reading the script from the slides.<br>- Some pauses are there during the presentation for dramatic effect.<br>- Structured the narrative, and thoughts or delivery are not disjointed. |

## Suggestions to Make Your Project Stand Out

1. Deliver a partnerships slide discussing which 3rd party partners this product may want to connect with.
2. Deliver a Go-To-Market slide discussing how the product will be introduced to the market and the characteristics of the ideal customer.
