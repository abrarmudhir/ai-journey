### Validate — Write a Research Plan

**Outcome: Passed.** *Screenshots 23–24.*

The reviewer described the plan as detailed and thorough, with suitable participant selection, relevant open-ended questions, and a friendly interview tone.

Rubric expectations:

- Identify the target user and screening characteristics, such as new versus existing users.
- Structure background questions to build rapport and understand the participant's fit with the target user.
- Start tasks with a scenario and a user goal, then ask follow-up questions about specific flow components.
- Maintain a friendly tone and explain what to expect.
- Ask open-ended questions and avoid leading questions such as “How easy is this?”
- Make natural transitions between sections and tasks.
